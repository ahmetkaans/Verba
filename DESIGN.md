## Style: "clean study app"

- Calm, focused and minimal, but not boring. For adults, teens and kids.
- Neutral surfaces everywhere; color comes only from the **level accent** and from the **correct / wrong** states. The one exception is the optional **Halloween theme** (Trick or Treat), a whole separate look: see "Light and dark mode".
- **"Warm paper" redesign (owner chose it from three directions, 2026-11-19; first round = shared style, level screen, quiz screen; other pages follow in their own plans):** warm cream cards (`.card`, menu buttons) on a neutral cream page, hairline borders (1.5px on buttons and answer options), soft shadows, rounded corners (16px cards, 12px controls). A level card has a 5px stripe on its left edge in its level color (owner chose the stripe from five mock-ups, 2026-11-19; the first idea, a short bar, was dropped), the code in 600 and the name small and muted; a small teal dot sits before "Verba" on the level screen. Multiple Choice options carry letters A to D (CSS counters, not on True or False); a right or wrong option also shows a check or a cross drawn in CSS, so color is never the only cue.
- **Menu screens and results (second round of the redesign, 2026-11-19):** category, topic and question type cards are plain cards with a 600 label and no marker (a marker only ever means the level: the stripe on level cards and History rows); the Difficulty cards show 1 to 4 small dots (Easy to Very Hard, by position, drawn with CSS gradients, no extra element). The breadcrumb is 0.85rem. The round top bar buttons have the soft shadow. On the results page the score is 4rem / 600, the hero card has a 4px accent line on top, and each question card keeps its colored left stripe and adds a mark in the top right corner (check = correct, cross = incorrect, dot = partly right), so color is never the only cue.
- **Pop-ups, Settings and the menu tree (third round, 2026-11-19):** pop-up corners 20px, titles 600. Settings is one list separated by hairlines (no box around each setting). The accent on screens with no level chosen (Settings, pop-ups from the main menu) is the warm ink above, so the selected buttons and the switches are dark brown on cream and cream on graphite.
- **History and Word list (fourth round, 2026-11-19):** the overall result number is 2.8rem / 600 and its card has the same 4px accent line as the results hero; each play's score is 1.8rem / 600. In a play's row and in the results summary the **Correct** pill has a light green tint and the **Incorrect** pill a light red tint (CSS by position: Correct is always first, Incorrect always third from last; the words stay, so color is not the only cue; still only for answers). The difficulty pills of the overall block keep the accent tint. Word list: the word itself is 600. Each History row (normal, perfect and quit) also shows the play's level as a 5px stripe on its left edge, in the level color (`js/history-screen.js` sets `data-level`, `css/history.css` maps it to `--lvl-A1` ... `--lvl-C1`; quit rows at half strength; owner approved the small JavaScript change, 2026-11-19).
- **Fill in the Blanks, Matching and disabled buttons (fifth round, 2026-11-19):** after Check, the typed blank (and the SmartBoard blank) carries a mark at its right end: a check (right), a cross (wrong) or a dot (partly right), drawn with CSS gradients from the theme colors (no image, no data address), with right padding so text never goes under it. The word bank is a quiet tint with a 1px line. In Matching, after Check a right pair has a solid border and a green number badge, a wrong pair a dashed border and a red badge (shape as well as color); the badge has a 2px ring so it no longer eats the border. A disabled main button (Check before an answer) is a flat quiet fill with muted text at full opacity instead of a half-faded accent.
- (Older text, kept for history: soft white cards on a page lightly tinted with the level color, 14px and 10px corners.)
- Works on phones and computers: single centered column (`max-width: 760px`, 16px side gutter), all tap targets at least 44–48px tall, menu grids are 2 columns on phones and auto-fill on wider screens, primary actions are full width on phones.

## Light and dark mode

- Follows the device setting by default (`prefers-color-scheme`).
- Theme is chosen in **Settings** with three buttons, **Auto / Light / Dark** (a segmented control). The choice is remembered in `localStorage` (`lg-theme`); a tiny script in `<head>` applies it before the first paint to avoid a flash.
- **Halloween theme** (Trick or Treat in Settings; owner's request, 2026-11-03; `css/halloween.css`): a whole third look that **replaces Auto / Light / Dark while it is on** (off by default, remembered as `lg-halloween`; Auto / Light / Dark are grayed out in Settings with a note). Chosen by the owner from sketches: **AMOLED black with thin orange lines**. Page and cards `#000000`; text `#F2F2F2` / `#B8B8B8`; **one pumpkin orange `#FF8A1F` for every accent** (the five level colors are five close orange shades, `#FF8A1F #FF9A3C #FF7A00 #FFA94D #FFB347`, only so the confetti has some variety); dim amber lines `#5A3A14` for ordinary borders; green `#4ADE80` and red `#F87171` for right and wrong answers; no shadows; pop-up dim `rgba(0,0,0,.82)`. - Tokens are defined on `:root` (light), redefined under `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark) { :root:not([data-theme="light"]) }` and under `:root[data-theme="dark"]`. Only token values change between modes; layout, spacing and motion stay the same so the calm feel is identical.

## Typography

- **Inter** from Google Fonts, weights **400 (regular)**, **500 (medium)** and **600 (semibold, since the 2026-11-19 redesign: titles, level codes, the question, the Word list button)**, `display=swap`.
- Fallback: the device's system font (`system-ui, -apple-system, "Segoe UI", Roboto, …`) — used automatically when offline or while Inter loads.
- Buttons use 500; body text uses 400; page titles (h1), the level code, the quiz question and the Word list button use 600. Nothing heavier (no 700). Numbers in the timer and score use tabular figures.

## Motion

All motion is short (≤ 350ms, confetti ~2.8s) and soft (ease-out, tiny distances). **The one exception is the Halloween walker** (Halloween theme only, see Components): a calm decoration that lasts much longer (one pass of about 25 s on a phone and about 57 s on a wide screen, then the 2.2 s sinking when a quiz starts).

- Screens and the feedback box fade/rise in by 4–6px.
- **Correct answer**: the correct element(s) do a gentle "pop" (scale to 1.035 and back, 320ms).
- Buttons press down slightly (`scale(.98)`).
- Low-time timer bar pulses softly.
- **Confetti** on the results screen for a high score: final score **≥ 80** (Multiple Choice, Fill in the Blanks, True or False) or **≥ 75** (Matching, which has partial points). Drawn on a temporary `<canvas>` in the five level colors, removed when done or when leaving the screen.
- **Menu tree**: the backdrop fades in (0.25s); on wide screens the drawer slides in from the right (0.28s, ease-out), on phones the sheet slides up (0.22s); then the steps fade and slide in one after another (0.06s apart, starting 0.12s in). Closing reverses it (`CONFIG.TREE_CLOSE_MS` = 280).
- **Halloween walker** (Trick or Treat only): one slow pass along the bottom per visit, drawn frame by frame by a script (not CSS); the feet, bumps, tugs, look-back, rope and the 2.2 s sinking into the ground are described under Components. It never moves faster than a calm walk (28 px per second), it lives behind everything, and it never blocks a tap.

## Sound

Generated with the Web Audio API in `game/js/sound.js` (no audio files):

- **Correct**: soft two-note sine chime (E5 → B5).
- **Partly right**: one soft sine note (D5), between the chime and the low tone.
- **Wrong**: gentle low triangle tone gliding down (A3 → F3).
- **Tick**: very quiet short sine blip once per second in the last 5 seconds of the normal timer and of the extra time.
- Sound is **on by default**. The Sound switch in **Settings** remembers the choice in `localStorage` (`lg-muted`).
