## Proposed wording for rules 10 and 16

Add to `AGENTS.md` rule 10 and the matching rule in `docs/RULES_FULL.md`, unchanged in meaning from the current text:

> **Root cause first.** When a script fails, a check catches an error, or a step costs extra effort, find the cause before patching. Then propose a backward-compatible guard that stops the whole class of error: a new check plus a self-test (rule 13), a tightened rule, or a structural guard. Never weaken a check, a cap or a CEFR ceiling to make something pass. Wait for the owner's go-ahead.
>
> **Spotted while working.** While reading files the task already needs (no extra exploratory reads), note any stale path or doc, inconsistency, or clear token or speed gain in a short "Spotted while working" note at handoff. Touch nothing outside the active task until the owner approves.

For rule 16(b), the lesson file would read: "root-cause rule: here and in RULES_FULL (rule 11)".
